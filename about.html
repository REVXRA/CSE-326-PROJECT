About LabBook

LabBook is a Digital Lab Equipment Booking system made as a CSE 326 course project. It helps students, faculty and lab staff share academic laboratory equipment without booking clashes.

Why LabBook?

Shared lab equipment is often booked on paper or by word of mouth. This leads to double bookings, missed slots and no record of who used what. LabBook puts everything in one place.

Main Features

  • Equipment catalogue with lab, category, quantity and status
  • Availability check by equipment and date
  • Booking requests with conflict prevention
  • Approval workflow for faculty and lab in-charges
  • Usage history of every booking
  • Maintenance blocking for equipment under repair
  • Notifications for approvals, reminders and changes
  • Admin dashboard with reports

Who Can Use It

  • Students - browse, check availability, book and view history.
  • Faculty - approve or reject requests.
  • Lab Instructor / Technician - manage approvals and maintenance.
  • Admin - manage equipment, users and reports.
