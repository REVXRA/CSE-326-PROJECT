Conflict Rules

The system checks every request before it is sent:

  • The time slot must not overlap with another approved or pending booking.
  • Equipment under maintenance cannot be booked.
  • The start time must be earlier than the end time.
  • The date must be today or a future date.
  • Every request needs approval from the supervisor or lab in-charge.
