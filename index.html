How it works

  1. Browse the equipment catalogue and pick what you need.
  2. Check the free slots on the availability page.
  3. Request a slot using the booking form. The system checks for conflicts.
  4. Wait for approval from the faculty or lab in-charge and track it under My Bookings.
  5. Use the equipment at the booked time. It is then saved in your usage history.

Who uses LabBook?

  • Students - browse, check availability, request bookings and view their history.
  • Faculty - approve or reject requests for their courses and projects.
  • Lab In-charge / Technician - manage approvals and maintenance blocks.
  • Admin - manage equipment and users from the dashboard.
